About Kocheng Hosting

Making bot hosting accessible to everyone. Our platform enables users to deploy and manage Discord and WhatsApp bots without maintaining their own hosting infrastructure.

Who We Are

Kocheng Hosting is a hosting platform dedicated to offering accessible solutions for bot developers and users. It provides a simple hosting environment that enables people to deploy and manage their Discord and WhatsApp bots.

Our goal is to simplify bot hosting for individuals and communities that want to explore automation, develop projects, or keep their bots online without having to manage a server.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make bot hosting more accessible through an easy-to-use platform designed for users with varying levels of technical experience.

We believe technology experimentation and the development of useful bot projects should be accessible to everyone. That’s why we work to make hosting resources easier to access and manage.

What We Offer

Discord Bot Hosting

Hosting solutions for users who want to deploy and manage Discord bots across their servers and communities.

WhatsApp Bot Hosting

Hosting solutions for WhatsApp automation projects and bot applications that require a runtime environment.

Server Management

Our platform includes a server management panel that enables users to administer their hosting environments and track available resources.

Accessible Hosting

Our goal is to make hosting more accessible for users interested in exploring bot development and automation projects.

Our Approach

We aim to deliver a practical hosting environment while continuously enhancing the platform and its features. We recognize that hosting services require technical resources, regular maintenance, and ongoing operational expenses.

We believe that providing clear information is essential. Our policies describe how our services operate, how we manage user information, and what users can expect when they use Kocheng Hosting.
