About Kocheng Hosting

Kocheng Hosting is a hosting platform that enables developers to deploy and manage their applications using an easy-to-access web-based control panel.

Our services accommodate projects including Discord bots, WhatsApp bots, and other applications that run in our hosting environment.

The Pterodactyl control panel provides a centralized interface for managing servers, tracking resource usage, and controlling applications.

We strive to offer accessible hosting solutions for developers, beginners, and anyone interested in exploring application hosting.
